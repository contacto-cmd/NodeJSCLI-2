### Technical Implementations
- **OAUTH 2.0 / OIDC Identity Provider**: Active with endpoints for OIDC Core, JWKS, W3C DID Web, OAuth Authorization Server, Token endpoint, UserInfo, and an IdP Dashboard. Uses RS256/RSA-4096 algorithms.
- **Real Inverse Algebra Engine**: Implements Gauss-Jordan inverse for matrices up to 5x5, recursive determinant, trace, Frobenius norm, and singularity analysis.
- **Gravitational Field Calculation**: Uses the WGS84 model with Helmert's formula.
- **Quantum Chess**: Features 32 pieces with quantum mass, gravitational fields, and potential energy.
- **Topology Visualization**: Renders Poincaré, Groth Sheaf, and Minkowski 4D models, including a visualized torus.
- **EAGI Pipeline Enterprise**: A modular AI pipeline comprising `ingest.js`, `parser.js`, `decision.js`, `execution.js`, and execution modules for `deploy.js`, `audit.js`, and `amy.js`. Accessible via `POST /api/eagi/command`.
- **Architectural Physics Engine**: Calculates structural integrity based on real material densities (Titanium, Carbon Fiber, Bronze, Aluminum, Steel, Tempered Glass, Concrete), flexion moments for cantilevers, center of gravity, and structural safety factors (>2.5 for viability). It also computes gravitational force in Newtons and material resistance in MPa.
- **Governmental Certification System**: Generates digital property certificates with RSA-4096 signatures, SHA-256 hashes, market valuations, and precise GPS coordinates. Professional technical plans include detailed gravity calculations, structural weight, precise measurements, material specifications, cantilever analysis, and safety factors.
- **Vault Encryption**: Utilizes AES-256-GCM + RSA-4096.
- **3D Live System**: Features a Cesium 3D globe with 40 nodes and 8 satellites, displaying GPS markers for architectural designs in real-time.
